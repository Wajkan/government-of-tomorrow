# Government of Tomorrow
*"Fully transparent and true democracy"*

By creating a shadow ledger that mirrors how tax funds are spent, we can track every transaction to a specific depth and build a fully transparent government. Political decisions would be backed by real data in real-time — enabling a more trustworthy government and a more precise, dynamic democracy.

This would hopefully lead to greater public engagement in political decisions and bring clarity to how and why certain decisions are made.

*"The government is elected by us and works for us — not the other way around."*

There is absolutely no reason why citizens should be excluded from participating in certain decisions.

---

## Goal

A concept based on real tax fund spending in Sweden, designed to highlight the transparency issue within our governments.

## Key Questions
- Donations — How much is donated, and to whom?
- Military — How much is spent on defense?
- Undisclosed — How much spending remains hidden from the public?
- Healthcare — What portion goes toward public health?
- Political welfare — How much funds political institutions and officials?
- Public sector — Where does the money go?
- Public broadcasting — How is our mandatory fee to SVT & SR spent?
- Schools — What is invested in education?
- Immigration — How are immigration-related funds allocated?
- Unemployment — What is spent on unemployment benefits and related support?
- Work stress & illness — How much does work-related mental and physical illness cost society?
- Alcohol-related crime — What are the costs of alcohol-driven criminal activity?
- Cannabis-related crime — How much is spent on policing and prosecuting cannabis-related offenses?

*"What is the money used for?"*
